First and Second semesters are created automatically.
How the calendar works
  1. Make current — the whole portal works in this semester.
  2. Open registration — students can add and drop courses.
  3. Close registration — the course list is frozen.
  4. Mark completed — the semester is finished; registration can never reopen.
  5. Complete session — once both semesters are completed.
Sessions and semesters can only be deleted while nothing has been recorded in them.
